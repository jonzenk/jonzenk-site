The Live Show

JONZENK's live set is rooted in storytelling. It moves through sharp beat switches and stripped piano moments, shifting from loud, distorted rock/rap beats, cinematic orchestral sections to quiet, intimate ballads. Rap and singing sit over layered production, with live improvisation on keys cutting through groovier sections. The delivery feels direct and personal, turning the performance into a near conversation and creating an intimate, exposed atmosphere.

Who is JONZENK?

JONZENK is a French-American musician creating immersive, cinematic work rooted in storytelling. His first album WHERE DO WE GO FROM HERE was produced and mixed in collaboration with @Menflou.

His music video won Best Micro-Budget Music Video at the Toronto International Music Video Festival (2025) and was selected by several international festivals.

JONZENK's music is shaped by American hip-hop and cinema. With a background in jazz, he blends live musicianship with modern synthesizers and contemporary production.
